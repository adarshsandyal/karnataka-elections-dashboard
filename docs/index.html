Methodology & Elector Roll Field Guide

1. What is the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) 2026?

The Election Commission of India (ECI) conducts periodic Special Intensive Revisions involving booth-level officers (BLOs) conducting enumeration to register new electors who turned 18, record deletions of deceased or relocated citizens, and apply corrections.

2. Roll Status Categories Explained

  • Base (95.4%): The verified active mother roll electors carrying over from prior electoral rolls.
  • Additions (2.2% / ~1.01M): Newly registered citizens who reached voting age (18+) or migrated into the constituency.
  • Modifications (2.0% / ~907k): Electors with updated demographic details, corrected spelling, or address updates.
  • Deletions (0.4% / ~188k): Entries purged due to death, permanent outward migration, or duplicate voter registrations.

3. Understanding Electoral Geographies

Part (Polling Station): The fundamental spatial unit of elections. An Assembly Constituency is subdivided into hundreds of parts (booths), each typically serving 700 to 1,200 electors within walking distance of the polling location.

4. Gender Parity Benchmark (Sex Ratio)

Sex Ratio is calculated as: (Female Electors / Male Electors) × 1,000. Karnataka's statewide sex ratio of 1,016.5 reflects higher registered female voters than males, significantly above the national census sex ratio (~943).

5. Data Privacy & De-identification

Diagram Chasing parsed official ECI roll PDFs using OCR, systematically scrubbing all Personally Identifiable Information (citizen names, relatives' names, house numbers, and exact EPIC voter numbers) to create an open research dataset under the ODbL-1.0 license.
